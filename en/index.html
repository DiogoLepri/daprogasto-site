Frequently asked questions.

Does the app connect to my bank?

No. It never asks for your password or access to your account. You log expenses yourself, or export your statement from your bank's app (OFX or CSV) and import it here; the app categorizes it and skips duplicates.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone, in a local database, and in your account in the cloud, saved automatically a few seconds after each change. Only you can access it.

Do I need to create an account?

Yes. Your account is where your data is saved, in the cloud, automatically. Got a new phone? Just sign in and everything comes back. You can sign in with Apple, Google or email.

What does the chat send?

When you ask a question, the app sends a summary: the month's totals, categories, accounts, subscriptions and your latest entries. That summary goes to our server and to the AI provider, which answers. We don't store the question or the answer, only a daily count.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. With no signal, the app opens and logs just the same; when you're back online, everything syncs to the cloud on its own. Only the chat needs a connection.

How do I cancel?

In your iPhone's subscription settings or in the Play Store, at any time. You keep access until the end of the period you've already paid for.
